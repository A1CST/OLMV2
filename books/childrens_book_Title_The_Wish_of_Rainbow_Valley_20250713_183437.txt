“Wow!” Sammy squeaked, his tail twitching with excitement. “I wonder what I could wish for?” He thought about wishing for the biggest pile of acorns or the fastest legs in all the forest. But just then, he heard a faint whimper nearby.

Curious, Sammy followed the sound and found his friend Bella, the shy little bunny, sitting alone by a patch of violets. Her soft fur was matted, and her ears drooped sadly.

“What’s wrong, Bella?” Sammy asked, hopping closer.

Bella looked up, her big brown eyes shimmering with tears. “I wanted to join the other bunnies in the meadow, but I’m too small and too shy. They don’t want to play with me,” she sniffed.

Sammy’s heart sank. He remembered how much he loved being included in games and laughter. “But you’re special just the way you are! You have a gentle heart and a wonderful laugh,” he said encouragingly.

“But they don’t see that,” Bella whispered.

Bella’s eyes widened. “Really? You think that would work?”

“Of course! Let’s go!” Sammy said, bounding ahead with excitement. Together, they raced back to the stone, their hearts beating with hope.

Sammy thought hard. “How about a sprinkle of courage for you? That way, you can join your friends without feeling scared!”

“I feel… different!” she exclaimed, her voice filled with wonder. “I feel brave!”

“Let’s go show everyone!” Sammy cheered.

With newfound confidence, Bella hopped alongside Sammy toward the meadow where the other bunnies were playing tag. As they approached, Bella hesitated for just a moment, but then she remembered the warm glow of courage from the stone.
