One day, while Bobo was tending to his beautiful garden filled with shimmering flowers that danced in the breeze, he noticed something strange in the sky. A tiny star flickered and twinkled, as if it were trying to say something. Curious, Bobo leaned closer, his fluffy cloud chair creaking softly beneath him.

“What’s that little star saying?” he wondered aloud.
