He had a heart as big as the mountain itself, and he loved to create magical things with his fiery breath.

One sunny day, as Dazzle played with colorful clouds in the sky, he noticed a little bird named Pip sitting on a branch, looking sad. Dazzle swooped down, flapping his wings gently to land beside Pip.

“Why the long face, little friend?” Dazzle asked, tilting his head curiously.

Pip looked up with tear-filled eyes. “Oh, Dazzle! I lost my way while searching for the perfect nest for my family. I wanted it to be special, but now I’m all alone and scared.”

Dazzle’s heart sank. He loved helping his friends! “Don’t worry, Pip! We’ll find you a wonderful nest together!” he exclaimed, his eyes sparkling with determination.

“What’s that?” Pip chirped, eyeing the book with wonder.

“Hello! I am Biblio, the talking book!” came a cheerful voice from within. “I can help you create something magical! Just tell me your heart’s desire.”

Pip fluttered closer. “I need a nest for my family—a cozy place where we can feel safe and happy!”

Dazzle grinned. “That sounds wonderful! Let’s make the most beautiful nest ever!”

“Wow!” Pip exclaimed, his eyes wide with joy. “It’s beautiful!”
