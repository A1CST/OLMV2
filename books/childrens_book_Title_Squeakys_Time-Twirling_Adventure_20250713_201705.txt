One sunny morning, while digging through a pile of acorns, Squeaky stumbled upon something shiny half-buried in the soft earth. Just then, a soft voice spoke from behind him. “That’s no ordinary watch, Squeaky. It can take you anywhere in time!”

Squeaky turned to see Ollie, a wise old owl perched on a nearby branch. “Time-traveling? Really?” he chirped excitedly.

But as he frolicked, he suddenly spotted a shadow lurking beneath an enormous tree. It was a giant bear! Squeaky’s heart raced as he remembered his biggest fear—bears! He froze in place, unsure of what to do.

“Oh no! What if it sees me?” he squeaked, his voice trembling.

“Don’t panic!” came Ollie’s voice from the watch. “You can face this fear, Squeaky. Remember your courage!”

Squeaky took a deep breath and thought about all the times he had been brave before—like when he had climbed the tallest tree to rescue his friend, Benny the bunny. “I can do this,” he whispered to himself.

The bear sighed heavily. “I’m lost,” he said with a deep voice. “I’ve been trying to find my way back home, but I’m too big and clumsy for these tiny paths.”

Squeaky’s heart softened. “You’re not scary at all! I can help you find your way home!”

“Wow! You’re really good at this!” the bear laughed.

“I’ve had practice,” Squeaky replied proudly. “I explore every day!”
