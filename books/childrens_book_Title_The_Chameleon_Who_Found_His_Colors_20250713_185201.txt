Charlie nodded, but he wasn’t sure how to do that. Just then, he spotted something magical in the distance—a sparkling rainbow bridge arching high above the trees.

The animals began working together, gathering berries of every color—red ones for Ruby and her fox friends, blue ones for Bella and her bird buddies, and even some yellow ones for the butterflies. As they collected the berries, Charlie painted each animal with their favorite colors—red for the foxes, blue for the birds, and bright yellow for the butterflies.

As they prepared for their feast under the rainbow bridge, Charlie felt something warm growing inside him—a feeling of happiness and belonging.
