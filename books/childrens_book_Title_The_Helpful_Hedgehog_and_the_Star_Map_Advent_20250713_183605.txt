“Wow! Look at all these stars!” Hazel exclaimed, her heart racing with excitement. “I wonder if I can learn to read it!”

Just then, her best friend, Benny the Bunny, hopped over. “What’s that you found, Hazel?” he asked, his big ears perked up.

“It’s a star map!” Hazel replied, holding it high for Benny to see. “I want to learn how to read it so we can find our way to the twinkly stars!”

Benny tilted his head, his nose twitching. “That sounds fun! But do you know how?”

Hazel frowned for a moment. “Not yet. But I’m determined to learn! Let’s ask Wise Old Owl. He knows everything about stars!”

“Excuse me, Wise Old Owl!” Hazel called out as they approached. “Can you help us read this star map?”

Hazel beamed with joy. “Please teach me how to read it!”
