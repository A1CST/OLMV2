But there was one thing that made Mabel truly special—her bravery. Whenever someone was in trouble, Mabel was always the first to lend a paw.

One sunny morning, while Mabel was nibbling on a delicious acorn muffin, her best friend, Timmy the Tortoise, came rushing over. His shell was slightly dusty, and his eyes were wide with worry.

“Mabel! Mabel!” he exclaimed, panting heavily. “You have to help me!”

Mabel’s ears perked up. “What’s wrong, Timmy?” she asked, concern etched on her tiny face.

Mabel’s heart sank. Timmy loved that crystal; it glowed a soft blue and had been passed down through generations of tortoises. “Don’t worry, Timmy! We’ll find it together,” she said confidently.

Timmy smiled slowly, feeling a little better with Mabel by his side. “You really think we can?”

“Of course! With teamwork and kindness, we can do anything!” Mabel declared as she grabbed Timmy's flipper and they set off on their adventure.

“Have you seen Timmy’s glowing crystal?” Mabel asked.
