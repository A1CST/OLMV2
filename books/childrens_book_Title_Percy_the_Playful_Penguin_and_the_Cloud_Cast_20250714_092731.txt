“I wonder who lives there!” Percy said to himself. With a determined flap of his little flippers, he decided to find out.

As he waddled toward the edge of the ice, Percy met his best friend, Tilly the timid turtle. Tilly was always a little unsure about adventures, but when she saw the cloud castle, her curiosity bubbled to the surface.
