Intrigued, she used her trunk to dig it out. To her surprise, it was a beautiful watch! Its face glimmered like stars, and it had tiny gears that ticked softly.

“Wow! I wonder what this is?” Ellie said aloud, turning the watch over in her trunk.

Ellie's eyes widened with excitement. “Really? Can I visit any time I want?”

“Indeed!” said the owl. “But be careful. Each time you travel, you must face a challenge.”

But as Ellie looked closer, she noticed something troubling. A group of animals were arguing near a big oak tree.
