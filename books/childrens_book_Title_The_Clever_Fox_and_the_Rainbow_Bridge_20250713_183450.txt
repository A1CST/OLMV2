Every day, he loved to explore the woods, making friends with all the animals. But there was one thing Felix wished he could do more than anything else—he wanted to learn how to jump over the Rainbow Bridge that glimmered far across the stream.

The Rainbow Bridge was no ordinary bridge. It shimmered in all the colors of the rainbow and connected the two sides of the stream where the sweetest berries grew. Every time Felix watched his friends leap joyfully over it, he felt a twinge of longing. “If only I could jump like them!” he sighed.

One sunny morning, while Felix practiced his pouncing on a patch of soft grass, he spotted Bella, the elegant deer. She was gracefully leaping over fallen logs as if she were dancing.

“Bella!” Felix called out. “How do you jump so beautifully?”

Bella turned, her big brown eyes twinkling. “It’s all about practice and believing in yourself! Would you like me to help you?”

Felix's tail wagged with excitement. “Oh, yes! I want to jump over the Rainbow Bridge!”

“Let’s start small,” Bella said kindly. “First, we’ll practice jumping over this log.”

“Oof!” he exclaimed, shaking his head. “That didn’t go as planned.”

Bella chuckled softly. “That’s okay, Felix! Everyone falls sometimes. Just get back up and try again.”

With Bella’s encouragement, Felix stood tall. He crouched low, focused hard, and jumped again. This time, he cleared the log with a little wiggle of his tail. “I did it!” he cheered.

“Wonderful!” Bella clapped her hooves. “Now let’s move on to something bigger!”

Excitedly, they made their way to the Rainbow Bridge. The colors sparkled under the sunlight like a treasure chest waiting to be opened. Felix looked up at the bridge with wide eyes.

“It looks so high!” he whispered nervously.

“I can do this,” he whispered to himself.

Taking a deep breath, Felix ran as fast as his little legs could carry him. He leaped into the air—higher than he had ever jumped before! But just as he reached for the other side, he felt a flutter of doubt creeping in. What if he fell?

This time, his feet landed firmly on the other side! Felix looked back at Bella who was jumping up and down in excitement.

Felix's heart swelled with pride as he danced on his paws. “I did it! I really did it!”
