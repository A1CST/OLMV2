The bracelet sparkled like the rainbow after a rainstorm. Pippin picked it up and smiled. “This will make the perfect gift for my best friend, Flippy the dolphin!” he exclaimed, his flippers clapping with excitement.

“It’s a friendship bracelet! I want to share it with you,” Pippin said, holding it out proudly. “Together, we can spread joy all over Rainbow Valley!”

Pippin thought for a moment. “Let’s visit our friends and show them the bracelet! They can wear it too!”

As they continued their journey, they discovered something special—each friend added their own charm to the bracelet.
