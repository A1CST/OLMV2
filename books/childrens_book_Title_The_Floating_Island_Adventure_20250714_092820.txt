So off they went, gathering their pals from all corners of Whimsy Island. They found Mia the Magpie, who loved to collect shiny things; Leo the Lion, who could roar louder than thunder; and Tilly the Tortoise, wise and slow but full of clever ideas.

“Ready?” Ruby asked as they began their ascent towards Rainbow Falls.
