“Indeed I am, Bella! Have you seen the Wish-Granting Stone?” Mr. Bumblebee replied, zipping around the bright daisies.

“The Wish-Granting Stone?” Bella’s antennae perked up. “What’s that?”

Just then, Bella’s friend Leo the Lion Cub strolled over. He was small for a lion but had the biggest heart. “What’s wrong, Bella?” he asked, noticing her droopy wings.
