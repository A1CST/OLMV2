In the heart of the Whispering Woods, where the trees swayed gently and the flowers danced in the breeze, there lived a dreamy unicorn named Luna. Luna loved to explore her magical home, but there was one thing she feared more than anything—flying!

One sunny morning, as Luna trotted through the woods, she spotted a beautiful flying carpet soaring through the sky. It glided gracefully over the treetops, weaving through fluffy clouds and casting shadows on the bright green grass below.

“Wow! Look at that!” gasped Luna, her eyes wide with wonder. “I wish I could fly like that!”

Just then, her friend Benny, a wise little owl perched on a nearby branch, hooted softly. “Luna, why don’t you try flying? It looks like so much fun!”

Luna sighed, her heart fluttering like a butterfly. “Oh, Benny, I wish I could. But what if I fall? What if I’m not good at it?”

Benny fluffed his feathers and tilted his head. “But what if you can fly? What if you discover something amazing?”

Luna thought about it. She took a deep breath and decided to follow her curiosity. “Okay! But I need something special to help me.”

“Look, Benny!” Luna exclaimed. “This book has letters and shapes!”

Benny flew down to look closer. “Can you read it aloud?”

“Of course!” Benny chirped excitedly. “Let’s go!”
