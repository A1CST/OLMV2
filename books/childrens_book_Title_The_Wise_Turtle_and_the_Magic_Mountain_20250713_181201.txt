One sunny morning, as Tully strolled along the winding path, he heard a soft whimpering sound. Curious, he followed the sound until he discovered a small bunny named Benny, his fluffy ears drooping and his tiny paws trembling.

“What’s the matter, little friend?” Tully asked, kneeling down beside Benny.

Benny sniffled and looked up at Tully with big, watery eyes. “I lost my favorite friendship bracelet! It was made of colorful beads and sparkled like the sun! I can’t find it anywhere!”

Tully nodded thoughtfully. “Friendship bracelets are very special, Benny. They remind us of our friends and the love we share. Don’t worry; we can find it together!”

“Let’s think,” Tully said, scratching his chin with his webbed foot. “What if we imagine where your bracelet might be hiding?”
