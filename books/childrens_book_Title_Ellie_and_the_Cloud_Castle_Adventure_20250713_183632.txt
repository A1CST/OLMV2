“I must find out more about that cloud castle!” Ellie exclaimed. She rushed back to her friend Leo, the curious little lion. “Leo! Come quick! Have you seen that castle up there?”

Leo stretched his tiny paws and squinted up at the sky. “Wow, Ellie! That’s amazing! We should go on an adventure to explore it!”

“A Wish-Granting Stone? That sounds magical!” Ellie said, her eyes sparkling with wonder. “Can you help us find it, Ollie?”

“Of course! Follow me!” Ollie flapped his wings and led them deeper into the jungle, where vibrant flowers danced in the breeze and colorful butterflies fluttered about.

After wandering through twisting vines and climbing over mossy logs, they arrived at a clearing. In the center stood a large rock with shimmering colors swirling across its surface. “There it is!” Leo shouted, bounding forward.
