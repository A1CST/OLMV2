“It all started because of a misunderstanding,” the fairy explained. “We thought they were stealing our flowers, but they thought we were taking their magic dust!”

Hazel frowned. “That sounds awful! But maybe I can help!”

The silver-winged fairy raised an eyebrow. “How can you help? You’re just a little hedgehog.”

“I may be small,” Hazel said confidently, “but I believe that everyone deserves a chance to make peace.”

With a determined heart, Hazel took out her time-traveling watch and pressed a bright blue button. In an instant, she was back in her village, standing before her friends.

“What is it, Hazel?” chirped Pip the Pixie, her wings fluttering with excitement.
