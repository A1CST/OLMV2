Penny’s eyes sparkled with excitement. "Oh, I must explore that rainbow bridge!" she squealed, her heart fluttering with the promise of adventure.

“Why do you all look so sad?” Penny asked as she waddled closer.

The red fox sighed. “We were playing together, but then I accidentally knocked over the orange bunny’s toy car.”
