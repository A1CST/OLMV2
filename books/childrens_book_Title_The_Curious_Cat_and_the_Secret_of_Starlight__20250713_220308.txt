One evening, as the stars began to twinkle like diamonds in the sky, Whiskers pranced through the meadow. “Oh, how I love the night!” he purred. “The world is so beautiful!”

As he wandered, he spotted his friend, Bella, a cheerful bunny with soft brown fur and long floppy ears. She was hopping near a glimmering rainbow bridge that arched gracefully over a babbling brook.

“Hello, Bella!” called Whiskers, waving his paw. “What are you doing by the rainbow bridge?”

“I’m looking for the secret of Starlight Meadow!” Bella replied, her eyes sparkling with excitement. “I heard that if we cross the rainbow bridge at midnight, we can discover something magical!”

Whiskers’s whiskers twitched with curiosity. “A secret? I love secrets! What kind of secret?”

Bella hopped closer. “They say it’s a treasure hidden beneath the stars! But we need to solve puzzles along the way!”

“Oh, puzzles! I love those too!” said Whiskers, his tail flicking with enthusiasm. “Let’s go!”

As they approached the rainbow bridge, its colors shimmered in the moonlight—red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet. Whiskers and Bella looked at each other and nodded. Together, they stepped onto the bridge.

“Look at this!” Bella exclaimed. “It’s like we’re walking on a rainbow!”

Whiskers perked up. “Who are you?”

Whiskers thought hard and said, “A book!”

“Correct!” Riddle hooted. “Now for your next challenge: I am round like a ball but can be sharp like a triangle. What am I?”

Bella wiggled her nose. “Is it a shape?”

Riddle nodded. “Yes! What kind of shape?”

“A circle!” Bella exclaimed.

“Excellent! You have one more riddle to solve,” Riddle said with a twinkle in his eye. “I have four sides but am not very tall; I am often used in games and puzzles for all. What am I?”

Whiskers and Bella looked at each other, thinking hard. Suddenly, Whiskers shouted, “A square!”

“Well done!” Riddle clapped his wings together. “You have proven yourselves clever. Now, look beneath the stars to find your treasure!”

“What could it be?” Bella asked eagerly.
