Squeaky’s ears perked up. He had to see this glowing crystal! “I’ll find it!” he declared, hopping down from the tree. “I can save the day!”

“Don’t worry! I have both!” Squeaky chirped, puffing out his chest. With a wave goodbye to the owls, he dashed off toward the Enchanted Forest.

“Yes! I need to get to Misty Mountain,” Squeaky replied, glancing at the shimmering water. “But I can’t swim!”
