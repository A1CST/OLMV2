The bridge was a magnificent arc of colors that stretched over a gentle stream, leading to the Enchanted Meadow where other butterflies played and shared stories. But Bella was afraid. She often heard whispers among the flowers about how the bridge could make butterflies feel small and dizzy.

One sunny morning, Bella sat on a bright orange marigold, feeling a bit lonely. She watched her friends, a cheerful ladybug named Lila and a wise old caterpillar named Carl, play on the other side of the stream.

“Why don’t you join us, Bella?” Lila called, waving her tiny legs. “It’s so much fun over here!”

Bella sighed. “I want to, but... what if I get scared on the Rainbow Bridge?”

“You’ll never know unless you try,” Carl said gently, crawling closer. “Sometimes, we must face our fears to find new adventures.”

The colors beneath her feet shimmered brightly, swirling in shades of red, blue, and yellow. Bella felt a little dizzy but remembered Lila’s encouraging words. “Just one step at a time,” she repeated. She took another step, then another.

“What’s wrong?” Bella asked softly, fluttering closer.

Timmy felt braver with Bella by his side. “I can see the other side!” he said, his eyes sparkling with hope.
