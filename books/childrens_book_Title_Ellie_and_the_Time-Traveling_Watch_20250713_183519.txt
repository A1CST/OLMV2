“Oh, wow!” Ellie exclaimed, her eyes sparkling. “What a beautiful watch! I wonder what it does?”

Ellie gasped in amazement. “Anywhere in time? That sounds amazing! But… where should I go?”

Just then, Ellie heard her friend Benny, the little bluebird, chirping frantically from above. “Ellie! Ellie! Help! My nest has fallen from the tree, and my eggs are in danger!”

Without a second thought, Ellie turned to Tick-Tock. “Can you help me save Benny's eggs?” 

“Of course!” Tick-Tock chimed. “Let’s go back in time to when Benny built his nest!”

Just as they finished, Tick-Tock chimed again. “Time to go back!”

Ellie pressed the button once more, and they were whisked back to the present day in a flash of colors. When they landed, Ellie looked up at Benny's tree and saw his nest safe and sound in its new spot.

“Look! Your nest is just fine!” Ellie trumpeted joyfully.

Benny fluttered down excitedly. “You did it, Ellie! You saved my eggs!” He chirped with delight.
