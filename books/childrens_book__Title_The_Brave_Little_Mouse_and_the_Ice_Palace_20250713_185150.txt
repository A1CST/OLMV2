“Why is everyone so quiet?” Miko wondered, twitching his tiny nose. “I want to spread joy!”

Miko smiled wide. “With colors of blue and bits of magic! If we wear it together, we’ll remember to be joyful!”

The snowflakes surrounded him, their spirits lifting as they placed the bracelet around their delicate necks.
