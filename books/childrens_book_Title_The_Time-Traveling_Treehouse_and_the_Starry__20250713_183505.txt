“What is this?” Pippin wondered aloud, his eyes wide with curiosity.

Pippin nodded eagerly. “Let’s do it!”

“That’s right!” Tilly said, adjusting her glasses. “But we must reach it before sunset; otherwise, we’ll miss our chance.”

“Oh no! We must not be in sync!” Tilly said, flapping her wings in frustration. “Let’s try again.”

“Oh no! We have to catch them!” Pippin cried out.

Pippin nodded determinedly. “Let’s split up! I’ll catch stars from the left, and you catch from the right!”
