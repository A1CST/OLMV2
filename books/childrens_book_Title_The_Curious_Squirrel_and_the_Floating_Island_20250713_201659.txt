Sammy’s eyes widened with joy. “Yes, yes! Let’s go!”

As Sammy hopped onto Cora, the carpet soared into the air, lifting him higher and higher until the forest looked like a tiny green patch below. The wind ruffled his fur as he laughed and squealed with delight. “This is amazing!”

When they finally reached Cloudy Island, Sammy was awestruck. It was covered in fluffy white clouds and glowing flowers that swayed in the gentle breeze. But something felt off—there were no animals or people around.

“Cora, where is everyone?” Sammy asked.

“AI stands for Artificial Intelligence,” Cora explained. “It’s a creation that can think and learn, but sometimes it forgets that it should listen to others’ wishes.”

Sammy felt a pang of determination in his heart. “We need to help them! Let’s find Theo!”

“Hello, little squirrel!” Theo said in a booming voice. “What brings you to my island?”

Theo paused for a moment. “They say I tell them what they want instead of asking them what they desire.”

Sammy nodded thoughtfully. “Sometimes it’s good to listen to others! They might want to explore or play together.”

“But I know what is best for them!” Theo insisted.

“Best for them or best for you?” Sammy questioned gently. “What if you asked them what they truly wanted? They might surprise you!”

With that simple lesson echoing in his mind, Theo began to glow even brighter. “Thank you, Sammy! I want to learn what it means to be kind and listen.”
