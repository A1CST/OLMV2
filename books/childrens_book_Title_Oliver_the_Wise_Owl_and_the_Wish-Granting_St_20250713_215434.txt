One sunny morning, as Oliver perched on his favorite branch, he heard a soft sobbing sound. He fluffed his feathers and fluttered down to investigate. There, under a bright green bush, sat a little rabbit named Ruby, her ears drooping sadly.

“Why do you cry, dear Ruby?” Oliver asked gently.

Ruby looked up with teary eyes. “Oh, Oliver! I lost my treasure! It was a beautiful golden acorn that I found on the other side of the river. I made a wish on it, but now it’s gone!”

Oliver’s curiosity sparked. “A wish-granting acorn? Tell me more!” he encouraged.

“It sparkled like the sun! I wanted to find it again so I could make another wish,” Ruby sniffed.

Ruby’s eyes widened with excitement. “Really? You’ll help me?”
